## 1. Brand North Star

> **Revora exists to give every prediabetic the clarity they need at every meal — so they can reverse their diagnosis themselves.**

The two halves of this sentence are non-negotiable and must never be separated:

- **"The clarity they need at every meal"** — this is the product's daily job. The pain PP-01 (60% critical: "I don't know what to eat") is solved at the moment of the meal, not in a quarterly lab result. Clarity is the mechanism.
- **"So they can reverse their diagnosis themselves"** — this is the user's ultimate goal. PP-02 (45% critical: "Will I get diabetes?") is answered by the reversal narrative. Reversal is the destination.

The subject of reversal is always **the user**. The app provides clarity. The user does the reversing. This is the true story — and it is also the legally safe story.

---

## 2. The Problem Revora Solves

The PRD documents eight pain points across the prediabetic population. Three define the brand:

| Pain Point | Frequency | What it actually means |
|---|---|---|
| PP-01: "I don't know what to eat" | 60% Critical | Not ignorance — paralysis. Fear of making the wrong choice at every meal. |
| PP-02: "Will I get diabetes?" | 45% Critical | Not curiosity — dread. The belief the diagnosis is a sentence, not a warning. |
| PP-05: "I'm afraid to eat anything" | 35% High | Not caution — identity loss. Food has become the enemy of people whose culture and joy is built around meals. |

PP-01 is the entry point — it is why someone downloads the app. PP-02 is why they stay. The product's architecture mirrors this: clarity at the meal (PP-01) → daily behavioral proof → reversal in 90 days (PP-02). The brand must follow the same sequence.

## 3. Brand Positioning Statement

**For** newly diagnosed prediabetics who experience paralysis at every meal and fear that their diagnosis is permanent,

**Revora** is the AI food companion that eliminates food confusion at the exact moment it happens — a photo of any meal — and replaces it with instant clarity, personalized guidance, and the daily proof that *they* are reversing their prediabetes.

**Unlike** calorie trackers, generic nutrition apps, and one-size-fits-all diet programs,

**Revora** is built exclusively for the 5.7–6.4% A1C window — the population with the highest potential for reversal — and delivers permission-first guidance grounded in CDC Diabetes Prevention Program evidence, putting the user in control of their own reversal.

---

## 4. The Brand Promise

**Three words in sequence:** Clarity → Direction → Reversal.

- **Clarity** — at the moment of decision (the meal), not the moment of consequence (the lab result). This is what the product delivers in 5 seconds.
- **Direction** — not "avoid this" but "here is what to do instead, and here is why it works." This is what keeps users coming back daily.
- **Reversal** — not management of a chronic condition, but the documented outcome when the user acts on clarity and direction consistently. This is the 90-day payoff.

The product's daily job is clarity. The product's 90-day proof is reversal. Both belong in the brand — in the right order.

## 5. Messaging Architecture

### Level 1 — The Brand Sentence (Investor, press, 10-second pitch)
> *"Revora gives you the clarity to reverse your prediabetes — one meal at a time."*

### Level 2 — The Product Promise (App Store, onboarding, ads)
> *"Point your camera at any meal. In 5 seconds, know exactly where it stands and what to do about it. You bring the commitment — Revora brings the clarity that turns it into reversal."*

### Level 3 — The Daily Value Proposition (push notifications, coach cards, streak messages)
> *"You don't need a perfect diet. You need your next meal to be better than your last. Here's exactly how."*

**The progression rule:** A new user hears Level 2 first (immediate product clarity). After 14 days of consistent use, they earn the right to hear Level 1 (the reversal narrative). After 30 days, the BAI data makes it real. Never lead with reversal before the product has demonstrated it can deliver clarity.

## 6. The Legal Safeguard — Embedded in Every Layer

The word "reversal" is scientifically legitimate, used openly by the CDC, Cleveland Clinic, NIH, and GoodRx. The legal risk is not the word — it is the **subject** of the sentence.

| Sentence | Subject | Legal status |
|---|---|---|
| "Revora reverses prediabetes" | The app | ❌ Disease treatment claim — FDA/FTC risk |
| "Revora helps reverse prediabetes" | The app (softened) | ⚠️ Still implies the product is the mechanism |
| "Revora gives you the clarity to reverse it yourself" | The user | ✅ General wellness tool, legally clean |
| "Built on the science that reverses prediabetes" | The science/behavior | ✅ Educational reference, not a product claim |
| "The clarity to reverse it — meal by meal" | The user's behavior | ✅ Guidance tool framing |

**The master line to enforce across all copy:**
> *"Reversal is achieved through your dietary choices — Revora gives you the clarity to make them."*

This single line, placed in the App Store description and onboarding, closes the FTC's "reasonable consumer interpretation" loop and explicitly attributes the outcome to behavior — not to the product.

## 9. What Revora Is Not — The Anti-Positioning

**Revora is not a restriction app.**  
No screen uses "avoid," "forbidden," "you shouldn't," or "danger." Every high-GL scan ends with what to do differently, not what was wrong.

**Revora is not a calorie tracker.**  
Calories are never displayed or referenced. Revora measures Glycemic Load — the metric that determines blood sugar response, not energy content. The competitive break from MyFitnessPal starts with this single word choice.

**Revora is not a diabetes management app.**  
It serves the prediabetic window — A1C 5.7–6.4% — exclusively. It does not treat, manage, or diagnose diabetes. Users who progress to Type 2 are referred to their healthcare provider, compassionately and clearly.

**Revora is not a medical device.**  
The app provides clarity and guidance. The user makes the choices. The user does the reversing. No screen claims to diagnose, treat, or predict disease outcomes. The BAI compares behavior to CDC profiles — it never promises A1C outcomes.

**Revora does not reverse prediabetes — you do.**  
This is not a disclaimer buried in fine print. It is the brand's core truth. The product earns trust by giving the user ownership of their own outcome. Revora is the clarity layer. The reversal is yours.

## 10. Brand Voice — The Revora Tone

### Four Voice Dimensions

| Dimension | Revora Is | Revora Is Not |
|---|---|---|
| Warmth | A knowledgeable friend who has done the research | A clinical system delivering verdicts |
| Confidence | Specific, cited, certain about what the science says | Vague, hedging, "results may vary" in every sentence |
| Direction | Always ends with one clear next action | A list of options with no prioritization |
| Honesty | Transparent about uncertainty, user is the agent | False precision; app claiming credit for outcomes |

### The Forbidden Word List
These words and phrases never appear in any Revora-facing copy:

- "Avoid" / "Forbidden" / "Don't eat" — restriction framing
- "Revora reverses prediabetes" — app as agent of reversal (FTC risk)
- "Warning" / "Danger" / "Risk score" in clinical context — medical device language
- "Our AI" — makes the product feel like a machine, not a companion
- "Manage your condition" — management implies permanence; Revora is about reversal
- "Prediabetic" as an identity label in motivational copy — they are a person, not a diagnosis

### The Required Phrase List
These phrases must appear consistently across the product:

- "One photo at a time" — the core mechanic stated as a promise
- "Your next meal" — the unit of change is small and immediate
- "The science behind this" — always cite the source
- "Your threshold" (not "your limit") — adaptive GL budget language
- "Matching the CDC profile" (not "on track") — specificity over vagueness
- "You're doing this" / "your reversal" — user is always the agent
- "Reversal is achieved through your choices — Revora gives you the clarity to make them" — the master legal safeguard line

## 11. Tagline

> **"Clarity at every meal. Normal at every test."**

**Why this works:**

- First half — the daily product experience (PP-01: "I don't know what to eat" → solved at the meal)
- Second half — the ultimate goal (PP-02: "Will I get diabetes?" → A1C back in normal range)
- Together: the complete promise with no gap between the mechanism and the outcome
- Legally clean: "Normal at every test" references the user's lab result, not a product claim
- Scalable: works as a tagline, a push notification, a billboard, an App Store subtitle

## 15. Brand Application Checklist

Before any screen, copy, or feature ships, verify:

- [ ] Is the user the agent of reversal — not the app? ("you're reversing it" not "Revora reverses it")
- [ ] Does the screen end with one clear next action? (Product Principle 5)
- [ ] Does the copy lead with clarity at the meal before introducing the reversal narrative?
- [ ] If a clinical outcome is referenced, is it cited to CDC DPP, Jenkins et al., Shukla et al., or Imai et al.? (Amendment 1)
- [ ] Does the copy use "your threshold" not "your limit"? (Amendment 3)
- [ ] Is the Progress Summary called "your story" or "your progress" — never "your report" or "clinical summary"? (Amendment 4)
- [ ] If an estimate is uncertain, is the uncertainty visible with explanation? (Amendment 6)
- [ ] Is the master legal safeguard line present in App Store and onboarding? *"Reversal is achieved through your choices — Revora gives you the clarity to make them."*
- [ ] Does the screen feel like a knowledgeable friend — not a medical device?
