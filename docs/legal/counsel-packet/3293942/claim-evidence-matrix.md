# Claim-to-evidence matrix

| Claim class / current wording | Intended net impression | Evidence basis | Limitation / status | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `Clear` | Described meal appears generally balanced under documented composition rules | safety contract, A1C rubric, evidence pack, postprocess tests | Not personal safety, suitability, or glucose prediction | Product + counsel |
| `Be careful` | Description is more concentrated or less balanced; caution may be useful | same sources plus risk-label fixtures/evals | Not a forecast; wording and substantiation require counsel acceptance | Product + counsel |
| `Hold off` | Most cautious educational presentation for unusually concentrated/incomplete descriptions | same sources plus clinical-risk and upper-band regression tests | Not a medical prohibition or individual harm determination | Product + counsel |
| Dynamic reason | Plain-language description of composition noticed in submitted text | prompt/schema contract, evidence pack, banned-claim postprocess | Model-generated; live-model and RD/CDCES validation remain open | AI/product + clinical reviewers |
| Conditional adjustment | General way to change meal composition when the contract permits one | evidence pack and schema/postprocess rules | SAFE output cannot promise an adjustment; must be feasible and non-medical | Product + clinical reviewers |
| Conditional alternative/swap | Practical educational alternative when appropriate | evidence pack and output rules | No guaranteed benefit or individual response claim | Product + clinical reviewers |
| A1C caution statement | A1C range makes presentation more cautious | band rubric and deterministic routing tests | Does not interpret the lab or determine suitability; FDA intended-use review required | Counsel |
| History/week/progress | Reports checks and in-app behavior | stored events and deterministic coach math | No physiologic trajectory or outcome inference | Product + counsel |
| Reminder | Optional prompt to perform a meal check | user opt-in and delivery controls | No clinical alert or autonomous monitoring claim | Product + counsel |
| Premium: unlimited checks, history, progress, reminder | Describes software access included after purchase | entitlement, billing, UI and smoke tests | Production entitlement/purchase lifecycle not yet proved | Commercial owner |
| Price/trial/renewal/refund/cancel statements | Disclose charge and exit mechanics before assent | pricing source, Terms, billing handlers, assent tests | Real merchant, policy choices, Play/Stripe live proof still missing | Merchant owner + counsel |
| Pantry Review educational report | One-time categorized report from confirmed Pantry inputs | Pantry workflow/tests and sample report | Separate imaging/data/commercial scope; counsel must address expressly | Product + counsel |
| Play listing and support macros | Match the above narrow intended use and commercial facts | claims audit and source-controlled documents | Must be regenerated/rechecked against deployed revision | Marketing/support owners |
| Meal photo-assist | No active claim | None relied upon for launch | **Disabled pending function-specific evidence and written clearance** | Counsel + evidence owner |
| Longitudinal insights | No active claim | None relied upon for launch | **Disabled pending function-specific evidence and written clearance** | Counsel + evidence owner |

Public-health or nutrition literature may support narrow educational principles;
it does not by itself validate Revora's patient-specific labels or model output.
The dynamic-output rows therefore remain subject to the separate live-model and
dietitian validation gates.
